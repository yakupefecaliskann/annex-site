How the European Accessibility Act was transposed, by member state

The EAA is a directive, so it does not apply directly. Each member state had to write it into national law, and it is that national law a business is actually judged against. This page lists the transposing instrument per country, taken from the official record.

  • Directive 32019L0882
  • Transposition deadline 2022-06-28
  • Applies from 2025-06-28

Why the national instrument is the one that matters

Obligations, enforcement bodies and penalties are set by the national law, not by the directive. Two shops with identical websites can face different procedures in different member states. If you sell into several countries, the relevant text is the one in each market you sell to.

Notes on individual states

  • Belgium Belgium has no single transposing act. EUR-Lex lists 21 measures across federal and regional instruments, several of them explicitly labelled partial transposition (gedeeltelijke omzetting).
  • Finland Finland split the transposition: the products act above plus government decrees covering services (179/2023, 181/2023). The Åland Islands apply it through separate provincial legislation.
  • France France transposed by amending its existing disability equality law rather than by passing a single new act.
  • Germany EUR-Lex lists 59 measures for Germany, including Länder-level instruments.
  • Latvia Supplemented by Cabinet Regulation No. 128 of 27 February 2024 setting the detailed accessibility requirements.
  • Luxembourg Amended by the Loi du 29 août 2023.
  • Netherlands Detailed requirements sit in a series of 2024 Besluiten covering products, audiovisual media access, e-books, transport, financial services and electronic communications.
  • Slovenia Amended by ZDPSI-A; detailed rules are set in accompanying Pravilniki, including one on assessing disproportionate burden.
  • Sweden Supplemented by Förordning (2023:676) and sector regulations from PTS, the Media Authority and the Agency for Accessible Media.

What is deliberately missing

All 27 member states above were read from the official record on 2026-09-03. Where a state transposed through several instruments rather than one act, that is stated in the notes rather than hidden behind a single title.

No penalty amounts appear anywhere on this page. Figures circulating in secondary sources contradict each other. Penalties are set per member state and can only be stated after reading the national text. We would rather leave the column out than print a number a reader might rely on.

We also do not name the enforcement authority per country. That one is not an omission we can simply fill in: the directive puts the duty to publish on each member state rather than on the Commission, so no single EU register of them exists. The enforcement page explains how that works.

The authoritative list is maintained by the EU: national transposition measures for 32019L0882 on EUR-Lex.
